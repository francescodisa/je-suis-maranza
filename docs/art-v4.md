## Prompt esatti

### streets

Use case: stylized-concept. Production game art for original satirical side-scrolling beat-em-up Je suis Maranza. Create ONE landscape sprite atlas of THREE equal horizontal strips stacked vertically, total aspect ratio 1:1, each strip a 3:1 panoramic background. Input image 1 is STYLE AND CAMERA REFERENCE ONLY; create entirely NEW locations. Consistent meticulously hand-placed pixel art, large legible pixel clusters, 8-bit aesthetic with detailed arcade Final Fight era architecture, dark outlines, limited warm amber / navy / muted turquoise palette. Row 1: ordinary Italian commercial boulevard at dusk, electronics shop, modest café, bakery shutters, news kiosk, bus stop, worn pavement; moderately prosperous, no opulence. Row 2: exclusive art-deco Grand Hotel frontage at violet twilight, cream marble, bronze revolving doors, burgundy awning, trimmed hedges, bellhop luggage carts beyond windows, chandeliers; substantially richer than row 1. Row 3: billionaire private marina by night, huge white luxury yachts and art-deco yacht club behind low railing, black blue water reflecting amber, high-end waterfront architecture and helipad distant. Camera side-on horizontal, buildings mostly front elevation, same horizontal ground line. In EACH strip architectural elements occupy top 72%, lower 28% is clear empty wide foreground street or paved waterfront for playable sprites. Entire sheet edge-to-edge, no gutters, no white margins, no text captions, no UI, no characters, no vehicles in foreground, no foreground objects or repeated tiles. Distinct intricate architecture, beautiful pixel clusters, controlled detail and clear silhouettes. Three complete separate scenes, crisp pixel texture, no blur, no 3D render.

Top-right: outside imposing marble Grand Hotel under violet night sky, protagonists foreground looking at suited elite guests behind red velvet rope, glittering chandelier, expensive angular steel Cybertruck parked beside portico, security guards. Bottom-left: protagonists and workers gathered at billionaire yacht marina at blue night, enormous white yacht towers behind them, a cleaner in working clothes points to yacht, rich warm lights, military guards at private gate, sea reflections. Bottom-right: dramatic cartoon rescue scene, Nora reaches toward a fellow protester held by two helmeted police beside blue police van, blue tracksuit hero approaching to help, no blood or injury, theatrical visual storytelling, warm orange city lamps. All panels complete beautifully composed images; preserve consistent faces clothing and style.
